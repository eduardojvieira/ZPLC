# Boolean logic in Ladder: a two-hand guard

Draw AND, NOT and exclusive-or as contacts in series and in parallel, and enable a press only with both hands on the buttons and the guard closed.

## Objectives

- Read series contacts as AND, a normally closed contact as NOT and two parallel paths as OR.
- Build an exclusive-or (exactly one hand) from two parallel paths of two contacts each.
