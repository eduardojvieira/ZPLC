Run one fill, hold and drain cycle of a tank with TON blocks in rungs, a panel start and an emergency stop.

## Objectives

- Time one step of a process with an on-delay timer (TON), using one timer for each step.
- Use a timer as a safety limit on a step that normally ends on a sensor, and let an emergency stop abort the cycle.

## What the evidence covers

The grade runs the simulated tank in the simulator on this computer. It says nothing about a real tank, valve or sensor, its timing or its safety.

## On a board

The tank, the valves and the emergency stop that the contacts and coils of this timed cycle read and drive are simulated; a learner board has no tank to fill, so the three TON blocks would time nothing. Complete it in Simulation, where the tank, its valves and the emergency stop are part of the scenario.
